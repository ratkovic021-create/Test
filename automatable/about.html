About Us

We believe your team's time is too valuable for manual work

Automatable was founded to solve one problem: brilliant people wasting hours on tasks that software should be doing.

Our Mission

Give businesses back the one resource they can't get more of

Every business has processes that someone is doing manually right now — copy-pasting data, writing the same emails, generating the same reports. We exist to end that.

We're not a software company. We're an automation agency — which means we take full ownership of understanding your business, designing the solution, and delivering it working. You don't need to know what Make or n8n is. That's our job.

Our clients get back an average of 40 hours per week. They use that time to grow, to think, and to do the work they actually started their business to do.

Our Values

How we work

The principles behind every project we take on.

🎯

Outcome obsessed

We measure success by your ROI, not by the number of automations we ship. If it's not saving you time or making you money, we haven't done our job.

🔍

Deep discovery first

We spend more time understanding your business than most agencies spend on the whole project. You can't automate what you don't understand.

🚀

Speed without shortcuts

We move fast because we've done this many times. But we never skip testing, documentation, or handover. Speed and quality aren't opposites.

🤝

Long-term partnerships

Most of our clients come back for more. We're not interested in one-off projects — we want to be the automation team you grow with for years.

📖

We teach, not just deliver

Every project includes full documentation and training. You should understand what we built and be able to make basic changes without calling us.

💬

Radical transparency

We tell you what's possible, what isn't, and how long it really takes. No overselling, no hidden complexity, no surprise invoices.
